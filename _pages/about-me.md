    <span class="when">How I work</span>
    <span class="what">Mechanical design and new solutions</span>
    <p>I am drawn to mechanical design: working out how parts should move, then using math and physics to make that motion better. What I enjoy most is taking a hard engineering problem, thinking it through and imagining a solution, then building and testing it. In my MS research, I took one idea from a first sketch to a working apparatus: a new, non-intrusive method for measuring solid circulation rate, developed from scratch.</p>
  </li>

    <span class="when">2026 to now</span>
    <span class="what">PhD student, University of Oklahoma</span>
    <p>Mewbourne School of Petroleum and Geological Engineering, with Dr. Baharak Sajjadi. Research: plasma catalytic hydrogen production.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <span class="when">2024 to 2026</span>
    <span class="what">M.S., University of Oklahoma</span>
    <p>With Dr. Baharak Sajjadi, I built a cold flow dual fluidized bed rig from scratch, set up its sensors and wrote the Python code that turns raw signals into results. Gary Stowe helped me build the reactor, and Jeff McCaskill helped me get the circulation materials. I was also a teaching assistant for Statics and Dynamics, Rock Properties Lab and Reservoir Engineering II.</p>
  </li>
  <li>
    <span class="when">2020 to 2024</span>
    <span class="what">Wellsite geologist, SOCAR AQS</span>
    <p>Company wellsite geologist on offshore high pressure, high temperature and high-angle wells. Performed real-time geological evaluation from drill cuttings, mud logging and LWD data, picked formation tops, and communicated changes in formation properties to the drilling and mud service companies to inform mud weight and mud parameter decisions. Supervised contractor mud logging, LWD and wireline operations and quality-checked subsurface data and reports. Gunashli and West Absheron fields offshore Azerbaijan, and the Tuzgol gas storage wells in Turkey.</p>
