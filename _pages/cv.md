## Research experience

**Graduate Researcher**, University of Oklahoma, Norman, OK
<br>August 2024 to present

- Developed a continuous, non-intrusive method for measuring solid circulation rate (SCR) in a cold-flow dual circulating fluidized bed for chemical looping combustion and clean hydrogen production.
- Set up sensor data acquisition (DAQ) with pressure and flow sensors in LabVIEW, and wrote the Python data analysis code from raw signals to final figures.
- Designed and ran 72 steady-state test conditions across four Geldart Group B silica sand types, bed inventories of 2 to 6 kg and air flowrates of 50 to 500 L/min.
- Current Ph.D. work: plasma catalytic hydrogen production.
