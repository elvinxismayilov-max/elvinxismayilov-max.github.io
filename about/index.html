About

  1. How I work Mechanical design and new solutions

    I am drawn to mechanical design: working out how parts should move, then using math and physics to make that motion better. What I enjoy most is taking a hard engineering problem, thinking it through and imagining a solution, then building and testing it. In my MS research, I took one idea from a first sketch to a working apparatus: a new, non-intrusive method for measuring solid circulation rate, developed from scratch.

  2. 2026 to now PhD student, University of Oklahoma

    Mewbourne School of Petroleum and Geological Engineering, with Dr. Baharak Sajjadi. Research: plasma catalytic hydrogen production.

  3. 2024 to 2026 M.S., University of Oklahoma

    With Dr. Baharak Sajjadi, I built a cold flow dual fluidized bed rig from scratch, set up its sensors and wrote the Python code that turns raw signals into results. Gary Stowe helped me build the reactor, and Jeff McCaskill helped me get the circulation materials. I was also a teaching assistant for Statics and Dynamics, Rock Properties Lab and Reservoir Engineering II.

  4. 2020 to 2024 Wellsite geologist, SOCAR AQS

    Company wellsite geologist on offshore high pressure, high temperature and high-angle wells. Performed real-time geological evaluation from drill cuttings, mud logging and LWD data, picked formation tops, and communicated changes in formation properties to the drilling and mud service companies to inform mud weight and mud parameter decisions. Supervised contractor mud logging, LWD and wireline operations and quality-checked subsurface data and reports. Gunashli and West Absheron fields offshore Azerbaijan, and the Tuzgol gas storage wells in Turkey.

  5. 2015 to 2020 B.Eng., Baku Higher Oil School

    Petroleum engineering, on a full merit scholarship.

  6. Childhood Building at home

    An arrow launcher with a release made from an umbrella button and a hook. Pulley systems that could lift my own weight. A fruit picker made from a pole, a basket and pruning shears pulled by a string. My favorite was a small hydraulic crane powered by medical syringes.
